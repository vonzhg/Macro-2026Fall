Assignments

What to hand in, every time. One PDF with your answers and figures, the code that produced them, and an AI_LOG.md recording how you used AI assistance. Every number you report must come from code you ran.

Homework 1 — Dynamic programming and numerical methods Available

15% of the grade. Part A out October 8, Part B out October 15; both due Thursday, October 22.

Part A — Dynamic Programming on the Computer Download PDF

The growth model on paper — Blackwell's conditions, guess-and-verify, and the stopping-rule bound — then the same model on the computer: grid VFI against the closed form, a grid-refinement study at two tolerances fitted against Santos & Vigo-Aguiar, and Howard plus monotonicity. Questions 2(a) and 2(b) can be done as soon as the sheet is out; 2(c) uses techniques taught on October 15.

Part B — Continuous Choice, Stochastic Shocks, and Accuracy Download PDF

Continuous choice by golden section or Brent; a second solver (time iteration or EGM) and a four-way comparison table; then a stochastic quarterly model — build the Markov chain both ways, solve, simulate, HP-filter, match moments, and report off-grid Euler residuals. Question 3(d) feeds the anonymous class accuracy leaderboard.

The leaderboard ranks accuracy reported with its cost, not accuracy alone. Submit your maximum log₁₀ℰ together with the grid size, the tolerance, and the wall time.

Midterm In class

20%. October 29, Block B — 75 minutes, closed book, no notes and no devices. It covers Sessions 1–5: equilibrium definitions and properties, dynamic-programming theory and algorithms, numerical methods worked by hand, and perturbation. A practice midterm with solutions is posted on October 22. The exam itself is never posted here.
