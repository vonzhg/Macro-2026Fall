Discussion

How this forum works — and who can see what
  • Who can read and post. Course participants only: sign in with your @ucr.edu address (instructors and guests are added by name). Nothing here is visible to anyone who is not signed in.
  • Named or anonymous, post by post. Tick Post anonymously and classmates see an alias such as “Anonymous Heron” — the same alias for you throughout one thread, a different one in the next.
  • Instructors can see who wrote an anonymous post, so that they can moderate. The one exception is the Anonymous feedback category: there the forum shows your name to no one, instructors included, and only instructors can read what you wrote.
  • Honest fine print. The forum's database stores the author of every post, so the person who administers it could in principle look it up. Anonymity here protects you from classmates, not from a database query.
  • Norms. Be kind and specific. Ask about ideas, errors and intuition; do not post homework solutions or solution code before the due date. Instructors may hide posts that break these norms.
  • Formatting. Markdown, code blocks (```python), and math with $…$ or $$…$$.
